# Grove Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Grove Launcher is developed by GraNet IT Solutions (CITARE HOLDINGS LLC). Questions about this policy can be sent through https://granet.tech/.

## Information Grove uses on your device

- Grove lists installed launchable apps to display its app drawer, search results, and pinned apps. Your layout and settings are stored locally on your device.
- If you enable Contact search and grant Android's Contacts permission, Grove reads names and contact details from Android's Contacts Provider for local search and actions. Search results are held in memory and are not uploaded or saved as a separate contact copy. If you choose a call or message action, Android passes the selected contact information to the app you choose.
- If you enable File search and grant Android's All files access, Android grants broad access to shared storage. Grove uses that access to index file names and paths in memory for device-wide search. It does not read file contents, change files, or upload the index. When you open a file result, Android grants the app you select access to that individual file.
- Turning either search feature off clears its in-memory results and stops new scans. Grove does not revoke Android permissions; you can revoke them in Android Settings.

## Reports, network requests, and retention

- Grove does not have an account, ads, analytics, or automatic telemetry. If crash reporting is enabled, up to ten reports containing diagnostic details such as stack traces and device/app version information are kept in Grove's private storage. Grove asks before opening your email app to send them. You can disable reporting and delete pending reports in Launcher settings.
- Choosing a Wikimedia Commons wallpaper downloads its image from Wikimedia over HTTPS and caches it locally. That request is visible to the destination service under its own privacy practices. Grove does not send your contacts or file index with the request.
- Choosing Google or Play Store search opens the selected external service or app with your search text. Those services then handle the query under their own policies.
- Local settings and cached wallpaper files remain until you change or remove them or uninstall Grove. In-memory contact and file-search results disappear when Grove stops or when you turn the feature off. Reports are retained locally until you delete them, open the email composer to share them, or uninstall Grove. Grove deletes pending reports when it opens the email app; whether you actually send the message is your choice.

Grove does not sell your personal data. Android permissions remain under your control in system settings. This policy should be updated if Grove's behavior changes.
